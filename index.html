About Me

I operate institutions—and build the systems that help them operate better.

I am an Owner and Executive Director of family-owned educational institutions and farms in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh, India. Since 2016 I have worked across IT, operations, institutional strategy and administration. My technical path began independently in 2010, broadened through networking, systems and security by 2012, and expanded into virtualisation from 2014. I approach difficult work through question analysis: background, foreground, prediction, timeline and possibilities before choosing a solution. My principle is simple: use technology where it is ethically right and operationally useful—not merely because it is possible.
