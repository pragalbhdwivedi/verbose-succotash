05 / What Changed

From technology enthusiast to institutional systems builder.

The strongest change over the last year is not a longer software list. It is the ability to reason across education operations, requirements, software, networks, infrastructure, physical access, surveillance and power as parts of the same system.

The portfolio therefore prioritises systems architecture, solution design, education technology, infrastructure automation and root-cause troubleshooting while retaining the full education, administration, curriculum, research, policy and operations foundation.
