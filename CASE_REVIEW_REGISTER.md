## Review rules

1. Do not refresh a date merely because the website was edited.
2. Review the underlying evidence and claim boundary first.
3. Change project maturity only when new evidence supports it.
4. Update `OUTCOME_REGISTER.md` if a new measurable outcome becomes available.
5. Update `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` when new media/source evidence is added.
6. If evidence becomes stale or contradicted, downgrade or remove the claim instead of preserving it for aesthetics.
7. Public repositories may be re-reviewed after meaningful commits/releases, not every trivial commit.

## Live display rule

The live portfolio may show a small label such as:

`Evidence reviewed · Aug 2026`

This label must not be phrased as `Deployed Aug 2026`, `Completed Aug 2026`, or any wording that confuses evidence review with implementation timing.
